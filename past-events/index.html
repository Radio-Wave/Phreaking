Past Events archive documenting talks, workshops, screenings, and performances organised by Phreaking Collective between 2025 and 2026. Events were presented through two exhibitions: BitRot (2025) and Does Cloud Compute (ever) Precipitate? (2026), both held in London. Participants and contributors include Lucia Rebolino, Terence Broad, Robin Leverton, Dian Joy, Erin Robinson, Anthony Frisby, Kat Macdonald, Cranberry Lemonade, Matt Hill, Margot McEwen, Melissa Li Yunzhi, Claire McMichael, Lagtime Seedling, Deposit Silk, Yiding Zhang, Orangespy, That Sounds Exciting!, and Algorave London. Films were screened by Lucy Ellis, Autojektor, Atay Ilgun, Maggie Maggot, Erinne Huston, Dana Fox, Shannon Lu, Verona Gryshchuk, Max Colson, Natalie Maximova, Isaac Ward, Tilly Hawkins, Ned Caderni, Iman Javaid, Julian Law, Ngo Chun Phoenix Tse, Mati Granica, and Yidi Chen. Topics span digital art, computational art, AI art, live coding, algorithmic art, experimental media, creative coding, internet art, new media art, media archaeology, internet infrastructure, and critical AI.
